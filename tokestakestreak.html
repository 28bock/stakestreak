Before you put tokens down

What counts as a workout?

A video of you exercising for at least the minimum time your challenge sets. Your opponent watches it and can dispute it if it looks off.

Do I have to be fit already?

No. Pick Beginner in the quiz and you'll get a suggested seven-day challenge with 15-minute workouts.

What are tokens?

Points you put up instead of money. Everyone gets 500 tokens when they join. Win a challenge and you take the other players' buy-ins. Tokens have no cash value.

Does the app cost anything?

No. The app is free, and so are your starting tokens.

How much do I have to put in?

5 tokens or more, up to 100. Everyone in a challenge puts in the same amount, and you choose it together.

What if nobody misses a day?

The tiebreaker you picked settles it: overtime until someone misses, most total minutes, or a split where everyone gets their buy-in back.

Who sees my videos?

The people in your challenge.

      The 24-hour clock

      When someone posts a workout, you have 24 hours to post yours. The window is 24 real hours wherever you live, and you'll see the deadline in your own time zone.

      Miss it, forfeit it

      If your window closes before you post, you're out and your buy-in goes to the players still in. When the challenge ends, the players still in split the pot.

      Rest days

      Your challenge can include up to 2 rest days a week. Using one adds 24 hours to your window, so you can skip a day without losing.

      Pauses

      Sick or injured? Ask to pause for up to 7 days. The challenge freezes for everyone once the other players accept, and nobody loses tokens while it's paused.

      Proof by video

      Each video starts with that day's proof code, said out loud or held up on paper. Opponents can see when the clip was filmed, and a clip filmed before your window opened doesn't count.

      Tiebreakers

      When everyone finishes, the tiebreaker picked at setup decides it: overtime, most total minutes, or a split. Overtime ends after 7 extra days with a split.

      Buy-in limits

      Buy-ins run from 5 to 100 tokens, and nobody can raise the stakes once a challenge starts.

      Keep it friendly

      Trash talk is part of it. Insults, threats and harassment aren't. Report a message and mute anyone from chat.

      Disputes

      In a group, the other players vote on a disputed clip. In a 1v1, you get 2 hours to film a new clip. If that one is disputed too, the challenge ends and you both get your buy-in back.

      Health first. Stop if something hurts. Ask for a pause instead of training through an injury.
